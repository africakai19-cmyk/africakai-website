About Us

Building Credible African Businesses

Founded in Polokwane in 2019 and serving businesses across South Africa.

Who we are

AfricaKai is a South African professional services firm specialising in business compliance and management consulting. We exist to translate African capability into structured, compliant and globally competitive business practice. We are the bridge between informal potential and the formal economy.

We are not a large, faceless firm. We work with startups, small businesses and entrepreneurs who need affordable, reliable, hands-on support, not corporate jargon. You deal directly with people who understand your problem.

Our story

AfricaKai was founded in 2019 by Madembe Maano in Polokwane, built on one conviction: African businesses, when properly built and compliant, can compete and collaborate globally.

“AfricaKai is not just building a company. It is building a model, a standard, and a symbol of what African business can become when properly built.”Madembe Maano, Managing Director & Founder

What we do

Business Compliance

Company registration, CIPC annual returns, SARS tax services (VAT, PAYE, TCS), UIF and COIDA, and basic bookkeeping and financial admin. We are the bridge between your business and CIPC, SARS and the Department of Labour.

Management Consulting

Funded Workforce Solutions: we help businesses host fully funded TVET students, onboard them into the right role and meet their obligations to them, through the Silver Bullet Internship Programme.
