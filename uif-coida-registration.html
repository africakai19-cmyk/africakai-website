Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

What are UIF and COIDA?

UIF is the Unemployment Insurance Fund. COIDA is the Compensation for Occupational Injuries and Diseases Act. Both are employer obligations in South Africa.

Who needs to register?

Generally, businesses that employ staff. Tell us about your business and we will confirm what applies to you.

I am about to hire my first employee. Where do I start?

Contact us before your employee starts. We can handle UIF and COIDA registration and, if needed, PAYE registration with SARS.

Can you do this remotely?

Yes. We serve businesses across South Africa by email and WhatsApp.
