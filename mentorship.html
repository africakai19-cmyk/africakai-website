The Programme

Mentorship Built for TVET Students

The Silver Bullet Mentorship Programme gives you mentorship, guidance and workplace readiness support alongside your studies. Open to all TVET students, from any field of study, with a special focus on funded students who want to connect with career opportunities after their studies.

How it works

Two sessions every month, interactive and practical:

Workshop session. Practical topics such as CV preparation, communication skills, workplace culture, compliance and job interview tips.

Mentorship session. Group mentorship, peer discussions and talks from guest speakers in different industries.

A note on internships. Funded students who need workplace experience may be considered for Silver Bullet internship placements with our client companies. Being part of the mentorship programme helps you prepare, but it does not guarantee a placement.
